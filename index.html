One panel, a full rescreen, or the frame?

Orlando pool-cage quotes mix three jobs. One patches a torn panel. One rescreens the cage. One rebuilds aluminum that will not hold spline.

Ask before you book: Is this price for the torn panels only, a full rescreen of mesh and spline, or frame, post, or anchor work — and who confirms the Orange County permit if the frame is in the quote?

Homeowners on public forums compare a few-panel repair with a full rescreen and a poly or Super Screen upgrade. Those are not the same square-foot price. A bent member or rusted fastener can turn a rescreen into structural work.
