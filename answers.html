Panel Or Frame Orlando Answers Disclose

How to ask

Get the scope in writing before you compare Orlando cage quotes.

  1. Name the damage: torn panels, roof mesh, bent aluminum, chalky paint.
  2. Ask for separate lines: panels only, full rescreen, frame or anchor work.
  3. Ask the mesh name and whether new spline, door kits, and screws are included.
  4. If the frame is in the quote, ask who files the permit and whether that fee is included.

Answers people ask out loud

Is a few torn panels a full rescreen? Not always. A shop can price the torn panels only. A full rescreen is every panel, including the roof, plus spline.

Does the rescreen include paint? Only if the quote says so. FullScreenFix publishes repaint as its own starting price. Pool Screens R Us lists painting as its own service.

Do I need a permit to rescreen? Do not guess. Orange County’s permitting FAQ says most structural work needs a permit and to ask Permitting Services if you are unsure. A mesh-only job and a new frame are not the same paperwork. City of Orlando parcels may not use the county desk.

Sources used for the permit line: Orange County residential accessory structures page and the Engage Orange County permitting FAQ, both on ocfl.net. Shop claims are only what each linked site published.
